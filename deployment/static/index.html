The Random Forest model was selected as the final prediction model based on its superior R² and RMSE compared to OLS Regression and XGBoost. It was trained with n_estimators=150, max_depth=10, random_state=42 on 80% of the cleaned dataset (n = 4,916).

🏆 Top 10 Feature Importances (Random Forest)

Importances are computed as mean decrease in impurity across all 150 trees. Urgency Level dominates — consistent with clinical priority triage protocols.

⏰ Peak vs Non-Peak Wait Times

Peak = Late Morning, Afternoon, Evening. Welch T-test confirmed a statistically significant difference (p < 0.05).

🚨 Mean Wait by Urgency Level

One-Way ANOVA (F-test) confirmed significant differences across all urgency groups. Tukey HSD showed all pairwise comparisons are significant.

The HMIS dataset (160 rows, 11 columns) from the Ministry of AYUSH covers outpatient attendance across Indian states. It was used to validate whether the simulated arrival rate is realistic for Indian government healthcare facilities. Telangana district hospitals showed higher λ values, confirming that our findings are conservative and real-world impact would be greater.
